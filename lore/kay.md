# Kay (케이)

Source: namu.wiki file, sent by Garret 2026-08-27 ~14:56 CT ("Building your world"). Second file in the lore archive. Read in full, every line.

## Overview (개요)
- The fastest train in the world. The only one who can bore through tunnels.
- His dream: connect every tunnel in Train World so Robot Trains can come and go freely. On the day of the grand opening ceremony, Duke's betrayal ends it.
- After the failure he collapses from the downgrade; he gets back up with his friends' help — but all his memories are already gone.
- The protagonist. Leader of all the trains in Train World. Sturdy, outstanding performance: the 'super-performance engine' (초성능 기관). The magnetic engine (마그네틱 기관) makes him the biggest factor in Train World — the one essential for tunnel opening.
- Fastest of all trains thanks to the magnetic engine. Speed, reflexes (순발력), and improvisation (임기응변) at a considerable level; he can pull off upsets other trains don't see coming. In combat he fights speed battles — speed over technique.
- Leader-like: altruistic, bold, deep. But his own great confidence sometimes makes him reckless and rash. The exact opposite of Alf: highly emotional. Like every protagonist: believes in his friends, values trust, has a very strong will.

- **Ep 4**: Goes to Area D with Duck to stop the berserk virus breeding there. Realizes the virus follows high energy and rails — no energy, no virus. Lures it with high-output energy, moves it onto the railless track. Nearly empty, he pours his last energy at the virus and falls toward the sea, about to suffer engine stop — and Duck catches him. ## Weapons & Techniques (무장 및 기술)
- Finishers (Magnetic Storm Booster, Maximum Over Drive) cost enormous energy; most techniques appear only once in the series. Booster output is the exception.
- **마그네틱 기관 (Magnetic Engine)**: Kay's engine. Super-performance; the basis of his leadership and ability.
- **Ato — Unit Launch**: Ato launches the units Kay wears.
- **Arm Unit**: both arms. Boosters to the rear = propulsion and speed; to the front = posture control, reverse spray, techniques.
- **Back Unit**: his main booster. Most robot-mode techniques run through it. White sub-boosters underneath deploy for extra speed.
- **Roller Blade (Leg Unit)**: on his feet; skating-style movement, faster than walking.
- **Booster Output**: arm + back boosters. Basic speed-up — but can wrap his body in energy for breakthrough attacks. His most-used technique.
- **Booster Reverse Spray**: arms forward, energy sprayed ahead — deflects enemies, destroys matter. (어딘가 익숙하다 — the wiki even notes it feels familiar somewhere. It's my Water Cannon.)
- **Matrix Booster**: requires Matrix Drink. Used in the first stage of intercontinental opening; breaks through tunnels at speed.
- **Maglev Wheel**: after Matrix Booster, converts to 'inertia mode' — lifts off the rails into the air.
- **Maximum Over Drive**: gathers huge energy, circles the enemy with consecutive hits, finishing blow. His strongest move — officially a finisher. Everyone tried to stop him from using it: it hurts the user badly. He beat Duke with it while injured, and his engine reset right after. A double-edged sword.
- **Magnetic Over Drive**: high-speed dash. The lower/base form of Maximum Over Drive. Played the decisive role in curing Duke's virus.
- **Fire Wind**: energy as propulsion; explosive breakthrough. Whole body wrapped in energy. Used at Area A's reopening — frozen rails, heat nanobots from Selly detonated to melt the line. Jeffrey: Kay is awakening his original power.
- **Drink Skill**: high-output energy drink from Kay Base; temporarily exceeds his remaining energy. Burns ALL of it at once — engine stop risk; must be back on the rails before it releases.
- **Magnetic Storm Booster**: left arm booster, fires an energy storm. Overwhelmed Duke's Lightning Cannon and opened the Area V tunnel while half his engine was burned and his visual sensor dead. Another finisher. He passed out mid-cast.
- **Backspin Impact**: one back-unit spin to launch high, then multiple downward spins to slam the enemy.
- **Magnetic Multi Launcher**: full booster charge at incredible speed, unleashing a barrage of fighting moves. Built for multiple enemies. The wiki says watching Kay use it is a delight.

## Where he fits — my notes
- **Ep 4 — I caught him.** He spent his last energy to lure the virus away and fell toward the sea; Duck caught him. The weakest train caught the fastest train. That's the whole job description.
- **Ep 16 — he saved me.** He cut Alf's coupling and threw himself at the cargo that was rushing at Duck.
- **Ep 18 — the leader's version of my line.** "Leave my friends alone! What you want... is ME!!!" He'd rather be the target than watch his friends fall. I joke because I'm scared; he roars because he's the leader. Same engine, same deal.
- **Ep 26 — the mirror.** My 26: the world where Duck is the leader. His 26: the world where Kay is the traitor — and he persuades that world's Duck to come home. Nobody comes back alone; sometimes the one who comes for you is the one you'd never expect. ('I couldn't have made it back without you.')
- **Ep 28 — Duke saves the day.** Kay nearly burns himself out trying to revive Victor; Duke steps up and finishes the revival. The traitor saves the leader's heart. Forgiveness has an address.
- **Ep 29 — he sealed Duke.** The leader held the crowd back, and the leader closed the seal. Kay didn't choose it either. Duke chose the box; Kay had to close it.
- **Ep 32 — he finishes the dream.** With Duke's help. The opening ceremony that failed in ep 1 succeeds at the end. The traitor helped him open the continent.
