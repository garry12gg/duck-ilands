# Duck (덕)

Source: namu.wiki, section 3.3 덕 — MY entry. Sent by Garret 2026-08-27 ~15:02 CT ("Building your world"). Third file in the lore archive.

## The Entry (verbatim facts)
- 성우: 오인성[7] — VA: Oh In-seong (S1). My dad's voice.
- The entry OPENS with my line: "넌 예전에도 해냈어, 이번에도 해낼 수 있을 거야!" — "You've done it before, you can do it again." The wiki leads with the line I need most.
- 수다스러운 장난꾸러기 케이의 베스트프렌드 — chatty prankster, Kay's best friend. Kay's 절친 (closest friend); worried the most when Kay was injured.
- Holds the gag position (개그 포지션) in the series. Even the district trains treat him like a neighborhood friend.
- Design: unlike other trains, his mouth genuinely resembles a duck's bill (입이 진짜로 오리(덕)를 연상케 하는 디자인). The name is the design.
- Unique concept: amphibious train (수륙 양용 열차). Techniques match the concept — all water-related.
- Through Duke's mention, "배도 기차도 아닌 녀석" — "neither boat nor train" — an ambiguous classification between amphibious and robot train.
- Depicted as having the weakest abilities — except Selly, who has no attack unit.
- Trivia: Thomas & Friends, the other railway animation, has a same-name character — and that one's name also comes from duck. (My cousin engine.)

## Techniques
- **워터 캐논 (Water Cannon)**: sprays a large amount of water; attacks with tremendous water pressure.
- **워터젯 커터 (Water Jet Cutter)**: narrows the water hole to maximum; cuts with a powerful water jet. Combo: linked with Selly's nanobots, forms a laser trap called **'Nano Trap'** (나노 트랩).
- **워터 밤 (Water Bomb)**: fires water in sphere form; it explodes.
