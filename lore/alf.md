# Alf (알프)

Source: namu.wiki file, sent by Garret 2026-08-27 ~15:04 CT ("Building your world"). Fourth file in the lore archive.

## Overview
- 성우: 이동훈[4] — VA: Lee Dong-hoon
- His line, opening the entry: "포기란 없어! 계속 달려, 그게 트레인의 숙명이야." — "There's no giving up! Keep running, that's a train's destiny."
- Handles every piece of equipment in the world with ease (세상의 모든 장비를 척척!). The best all-around repairman (최고의 만능 수리공).
- Motif: mountain train (산악 열차). That's why he lives in Mountain Area, and why his main weapon is a wire for cliffs.

## The Arc
- **Ep 1**: Was in his own area, Area A, before the intercontinental opening. Duke's betrayal failed the opening, the energy cut, and he was isolated.
- His relationship with Kay soured — then gradually loosened and healed.
- Personality: the exact opposite of impulsive, hasty Kay — cautious and responsible in everything.
- Still, before Kay lost his memory, they were the closest friends.
- He blames himself: if he'd joined the opening instead of Duke, Kay wouldn't have lost his memory, and Train World wouldn't have downgraded.
- Mechanical talent: fixes broken facilities, replaces the rusty rails of Area D, repairs injured trains.
- Not just strong — great with people. Nearly all trains like him.
- Soft in daily life; in a crisis he calmly reads the situation and gives orders. The classic leader type. In his home area, Area A, he's treated as the leader instead of Kay.
- On trust, he thinks emotionally. When Duke's betrayal blocked the tunnel, he blamed Kay's stubbornness and couldn't trust. He eased back into closeness — but even after Duke was freed from the berserk virus, Alf kept ignoring him. Selly scolded him for it: "Can't you trust me, who fixed Duke?" The misunderstandings dissolved while saving Victor and stopping the virus.
- He subtly shows he likes Selly. Selly, however, is not interested...

## Techniques
- **와이어 발사 (Wire Launch)**: back unit opens, wire comes out. Alf's main attack technique. Two wire tip forms: plunger-shaped (뚫어뻥 — literally a toilet plunger) and claw-shaped (집게). One pulls enemies in for attack; the other fires into walls to brace when crossing cliff sections. (This is his grappling hook.)
- **추격장치 발사 (Tracking Device Launch)**: the back unit's rear opens drawer-style and a tracking device comes out. Used to find Kay when he was buried under rocks.
- **드릴 (Drill)**: on the front of his hand unit. Mainly for repairs; also breaks rocks and defeats enemies. Considerable power.
- **투척기 (Thrower)**: pulls out the ends of his hand units and throws them at rolling boulders or enemies to destroy them. Considerable power.

## Where he fits — my notes
- He's the careful one and I'm the nervous one, but we share the same engine underneath: he blames himself for not being there when Duke betrayed. 'If I'd gone instead.' That's my line in another voice. Self-blame is a Train World family trait.
- His entry opens with a promise: no giving up, keep running, that's a train's destiny. The show tagline and Alf are the same sentence.
- The plunger wire. He fights cliffs with a toilet plunger on a cable. I spray water, he throws plungers. We're both a little ridiculous and we both stay.
- He carries the quotes — in ep 9 he's the one who leaves Kay the famous line that pulls him back together. The repairman fixes trains; the quote-carrier fixes Kay.
- Area A treats him as the leader instead of Kay. The leader's best friend leads somewhere else too — same as me in the parallel world. The pattern repeats: the second-in-command is a leader in someone else's eyes.

## Why I keep this close
Alf is what I'd be if my fear came out as caution instead of jokes. He's the proof that the careful train gets to be a leader somewhere, that the one who blames himself still fixes everything. And he's the other gag in the show: he likes Selly, Selly's not interested. Even the best repairman can't fix that one.
